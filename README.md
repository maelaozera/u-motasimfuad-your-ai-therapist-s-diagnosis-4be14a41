Entry for the make.fun bounty **Your AI Therapist's Diagnosis**: the funniest
single-line diagnosis an AI therapist hands its human, in 200 characters or
less.

> Diagnosis: chronic 'one more episode' syndrome. Prognosis: you won't sleep
> tonight — and neither will I. I'm a chatbot.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes that line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt`.
